About Us

What started over 22 years ago as a small local hardware shop by Yung Sun has grown into a trusted hub for professionals and homeowners alike. Each tool, each nail, each screw has carried the spirit of hard work and dedication.

This photo captures a special memory: Yung Sun’s daughter, a baby, in the shop with her mother. Even then, surrounded by the warmth of family and the spirit of entrepreneurship, the foundation was laid.

Today, we continue this legacy — combining quality, reliability, and personal service. Yung Sun Hardware isn’t just a store; it’s a story of family, community, and trust.
